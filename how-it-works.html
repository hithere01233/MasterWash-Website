How it works

First time here? It takes four steps.

Load

Put your clothes in a washer and close the door.

Pay

Pay at the terminal with EFTPOS or card, then pick your machine.

Wash

Choose a cycle and press start. Add detergent if you brought your own.

Dry

Move your load to a dryer, pay, and fold when it is done.

Common questions

Do I need to bring detergent?

You can bring your own, or buy detergent from the vending machine in store.

How do I pay?

Use EFTPOS or a card at the payment terminal. No coins are needed.

Is it open all night?

Yes. We are open 24 hours, 7 days a week.

Can I wash a duvet?

Yes. Use one of the large machines, which fit king-size duvets.
